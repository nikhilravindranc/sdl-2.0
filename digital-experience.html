Sample case study
Redesigning a self-service portal customers kept abandoning
Challenge

Customers were dropping off mid-task in a self-service portal, generating avoidable support volume and frustration.

What we designed

A simplified information architecture and redesigned core workflows, built around how customers actually complete tasks, backed by a reusable component library for consistency going forward.

Impact

Higher task completion, fewer support escalations, and a design foundation the team could extend to new features without starting over.
