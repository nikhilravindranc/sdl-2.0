A field service app rebuilt for offline-first reliability
Challenge

A field team relied on a mobile app that failed without signal, forcing workers to fall back on paper forms and manual re-entry.

What we engineered

An offline-first mobile application with automatic sync, AI-assisted form validation and a modernized cross-platform codebase built for reliable field use.

Impact

Fewer manual errors, faster job completion in the field, and a single codebase the team can maintain and extend.
